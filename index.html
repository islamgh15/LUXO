DECISION 01 · COUNTER MATERIAL
No precedent yet.
Decision made from scratch.
Oiled hardwood with sealed finish
OPTIONS CONSIDERED
MDF laminate (cheaper, fails under daily wear) vs. quartz (durable, high upfront cost) vs. oiled hardwood
REASONING
Prior cafe project logged laminate failing within 8 months under commercial use. Hardwood matched budget while meeting durability threshold.
HIGH CONFIDENCE · from 1 prior case
DECISION 02 · FLOOR RATING
No precedent yet.
Decision made from scratch.
R10 slip-rated porcelain
OPTIONS CONSIDERED
Standard R9 residential-grade porcelain vs. R10 commercial-rated porcelain
REASONING
R9 was specified once before for foot-traffic space; outcome logged a near-miss incident during a wet cleaning cycle. Never repeated since.
HIGH CONFIDENCE · from 1 prior case
DECISION 03 · LIGHT TEMPERATURE
No precedent yet.
Decision made from scratch.
3000K, commercial-balanced
OPTIONS CONSIDERED
2700K residential warm vs. 3000K commercial balanced vs. 3500K retail-bright
REASONING
2700K tested too dim for product visibility at the counter in the last project. 3500K read as cold. 3000K logged as the balance that worked.
